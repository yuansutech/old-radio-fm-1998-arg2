// Night Ferry · Midnight radio · 1996-1998

Before you begin

This is where I've organized the station's contents. Most of the recordings are already gone; what's left is only enough to piece together part of the story.

I'm Lu Chen, Night Ferry's sound engineer. Su Wan was the host. She liked broadcasting late at night, reading listener letters after two in the morning.

On the night of March 17, 1998, she went into the studio and locked the door. After that, she never came out.

If you're picking up FM 98.7 too, it means someone has reconnected this ferry.

I've put everything I could find right here.

Please help me find her.

— Lu Chen · March 20, 1998
