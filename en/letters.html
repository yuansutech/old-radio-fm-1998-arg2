letters.html —— Listener letters

Su Wan read many letters on the show. Below are the few that could be recovered.

Letter 1 · 1998-02-14 02:14

“Hi Su Wan. Tonight is the first time I've listened to your show. I'm working the night shift at a hospital; the ward is very quiet. You said, ‘people at two in the morning are closer to themselves than they are by day.’ I'll remember that.”

Su Wan read this letter on the show. She said:
“February 14, Valentine's Day. Today is also Night Ferry's first episode. I've made a note of this date. 0214.”

Letter 2 · 1998-03-10 02:30

“Su Wan, I'm leaving Qingchuan next week. Thank you for keeping me company these two years. When I come back, I hope you'll still be here.”

After reading it, Su Wan paused for a long time and said, “I hope so too.”

Letter 3 · 1998-03-18 03:00 (never aired)

“Hello. When I passed the studio, I saw the door was locked. Are you still in there?”

The letter was never sent. The postmark on the envelope is dated March 18, 1998.
