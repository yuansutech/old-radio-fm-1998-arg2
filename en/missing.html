missing.html —— Qingchuan Public Security Bureau

Name: Su Wan

Sex: Female

Born: 1972

Date of disappearance: March 17, 1998

Place of disappearance: Qingchuan Radio Station · Studio

Status: Not found

On the night of March 17, 1998, Su Wan entered the studio and did not reappear. Colleagues reported her missing the next day.

Police found at the scene: one tape, three listener letters, and a radio.

The tape's final recording runs to 2:47 a.m.; after that there is only static.

Several pages had been torn from the diary in her dorm room.

The case remains unsolved to this day.
