host.html —— Host · Su Wan

Su Wan, born 1972, from Qingchuan.

She joined Night Ferry in 1996 and hosted its midnight talk show until it went off the air.

She liked reading listener letters after 2 a.m., making the show feel like a conversation rather than reading from a script.

On the night of March 17, 1998, she went into the studio and locked the door.

After that, no one ever saw her again.

🔑 An interview recording (1997)
“People ask me why I chose the frequency 98.7.
There's really no story to it.
98.7 was the wavelength in the small hours on my birthday. I've always felt that people at two in the morning are closer to themselves than they are by day.”
