1998-03-12 · Qingchuan station to reformat midnight program

According to the municipal radio station, Night Ferry will be reformatted starting in April. Exact broadcast times are to be determined.

1998-03-19 · Notice on Night Ferry going off the air

For reasons personal to the host, Night Ferry will cease broadcasting as of this week. Thank you to our listeners for your support over the past two years.

1998-03-25 · Notice regarding host Su Wan

Ms. Su Wan has left the station for personal reasons and will no longer host any of our programs.

📌 Key point
The final episode was scheduled for March 17, 1998, but was pulled at the last minute.
Lu Chen was that episode's sound engineer. The tape is in his hands.
