This episode was never broadcast. The tape was found in a cabinet next to the studio.

[Recording begins]

…Night Ferry, Episode 48. The final episode as well.

Tonight I want to tell a story of my own.

In 1996 I came to Qingchuan. I brought nothing with me. Just a radio.

That radio could only pick up one frequency. FM 98.7.

I listened to a show that had already gone off the air, thinking: if one day I too could make a program meant only for people awake at two in the morning——

how wonderful that would be.

……(8-second pause)……

Now I know. Some things, once you say them out loud, there will be people who want you to keep quiet.

But I don't regret it.

If one day someone hears this recording, I hope——

it's not to stand up for me. It's to know that at two in the morning, someone was still speaking.

FM 98.7, good night.

[Recording ends]

🕰️ Aftermath
After this recording, the tape kept running until 2:47 a.m. Everything after that is just static.
