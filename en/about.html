about.html —— About Lu Chen

Lu Chen, born 1975, a Qingchuan local.

I joined Night Ferry in 1996, doing live recording and post-production editing. Su Wan was the host; I was her partner.

I did voice-over work for ads by day and worked the show with her at night. I didn't say much, only speaking during commercial breaks.

On the night of March 17, 1998, she went into the studio and locked the door. I was right next door. I heard her last sentence.

After that she never came out. The police said she'd left all her things inside and simply vanished.

If you've found this radio, it means I'm no longer around.
